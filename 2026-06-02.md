Three greatest achievements for SpaceX
1. Hired as avionics test engineer at Northrop Grumman in Elkton, MD, working on a solid rocket motor control system. Pacing ahead of expectations, I quickly became responsible for testing, taking over for three test engineers and earning three merit based bonuses for my work on the program. I quickly gained enough trust to execute almost all the remaining qual tests. I take pride in my attention to detail, speed, and problem solving needed to keep both development and operations moving on the program.
2. I designed, built, and flew an unusual flying machine capable of forward flight and hover without requiring more moving components than a conventional fixed wing UAS of its size. Its unique design is due to its reconfigurability, allowing the system to be repaired or modified very easily. The craft is manufacturable using a conventional 3D printer and common parts.
3. I built a playable checkers engine. The system was written in Python, only using Keras and Numpy as main external libraries. I sourced, processed, & cleaned training data to build a regression network capable of identifying winning positions. I mated the network to an alpha-beta beam search algorithm tuned to weed through the space of possible positions. The system played surprisingly well, considering it was trained on exclusively tactical positions.
